    Questions people ask us

    Is the estimate really free?

    Yes. We come out, walk the yard with you, take measurements and give you an estimate at no cost. There's zero obligation to hire us.

    Do I need to be home for the estimate?

    Yes, please. An adult owner of the home should be there so we can walk the yard together and answer your questions.

    How does financing work?

    We work with third-party lenders. If you qualify, you may be able to start with $0 down and promotional 0% APR terms for up to 24 months. Approval and terms are decided by the lender, not by us. We'll go over your options at the estimate.

    Are you licensed?

    Yes. We're licensed, insured and bonded. Ask us for our license and insurance details at any time.

    Do you do front yards and backyards?

    Both. We do front yards, backyards, side yards and driveways, as one project or a piece at a time.

    How long does a project take?

    It depends on the size of the yard and what you're putting in. We'll give you a timeline along with your estimate.
