Questions people ask us

Is the roof estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the roof and give you an estimate at no cost. There's zero obligation to hire us.

Who needs to be there for the estimate?

Please make sure all homeowners are there, so everyone can see what we find and ask their questions at the same time.

My roof is leaking right now. Can you help?

Yes. Book the earliest time you see, or call us at (760) 548-2781 and tell us it's leaking.

Do you only replace roofs, or do you repair too?

Both. If a repair will fix it, we'll tell you. We only recommend a new roof when the old one can't be saved.

How long does a roof replacement take?

It depends on the size of the roof and what's under the old one. We'll give you a timeline along with your estimate.
